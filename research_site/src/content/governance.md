## High-impact decisions need more than model scores

Credit approval, rejection and price affect access to money. Aggregate AUC or a working API cannot establish fair or lawful treatment. The repository records no completed protected-class, proxy or subgroup fairness study. {{evidence:fairness}}

## Required evaluation

A governed study should examine protected-class data under a lawful collection process, proxy variables, subgroup discrimination and calibration, error rates and disparate impact. Compare approval outcomes at a fixed policy with uncertainty and sample support. A demographic field’s absence does not establish fairness because other fields can act as proxies. These are proposed checks, not reported results. {{evidence:future}}

## Explanations and oversight

A borrower needs accurate adverse-action reasons, not heuristic differences from a median. Global importance does not explain a local rejection. Human review, an appeal path, documented policy, regulatory review and independent model-risk assessment are needed. The prototype’s approve/refer/decline strings are illustrative rules. {{source:predictor|Policy and heuristic indicators}} {{source:overview|Responsible-use limitations}}

## Data and monitoring

Collect only needed information, protect it, and define retention. Monitor input distributions, missingness, mature outcomes, calibration and subgroup policy results. No completed production outcome-monitoring system is documented. This prototype must not autonomously approve, reject or price real applicants. {{evidence:fairness}}
