## Read limitations alongside results

The registers tie each problem to an affected claim, severity, evidence, likely bias, mitigation and unresolved status. “Unresolved” does not mean hidden or unimportant. It means the record does not show a complete fix. {{source:overview|Recorded research limitations}}

## Main failure conditions

Do not transfer the model’s full-feature metrics to default-filled browser applications. Do not transfer resolved-loan probabilities to an arbitrary applicant population or outcome horizon. Do not claim XGBoost superiority from a zero-crossing interval. Do not call lifetime-loss-plus-annual-cost pricing a financial return. {{evidence:gap}} {{evidence:sample}} {{evidence:comparisons}} {{evidence:pricing}}

Timing errors, changed selection, cohort drift, subgroup disparities, incompatible artifacts and monitoring failure can each invalidate a different claim. Successful code tests do not remove these risks. Local HTTP evidence is not hosted VM evidence; a supplied recovery procedure is not evidence of execution. {{evidence:vm}}

## Resolution standard

Mark a limitation resolved only when its mitigation has dated, linked evidence, the affected claim has been reevaluated and remaining risks are recorded. Recommendations do not count as completed fixes. {{evidence:future}}
