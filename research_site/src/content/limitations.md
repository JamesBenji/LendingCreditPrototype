## A claim can fail even when the code runs

The research pipeline can return a probability for a row with all required columns while 40 of those inputs are training defaults. A calibration curve can improve while risk ranking deteriorates. A repayment schedule can balance to the cent while its rate has an inconsistent loss horizon. These failures affect interpretation rather than software execution. {{evidence:gap}} {{evidence:pricing}}

## The population and information must still match

Full-feature metrics apply to the evaluated research profiles. They do not automatically apply to the browser form. Resolved-loan probabilities also depend on the accepted-loan population and the snapshot's outcome maturity. If new applicants, field timing or follow-up differ, the estimate is answering a changed question. {{evidence:sample}} {{evidence:timing}}

## Uncertainty changes the conclusion

The XGBoost-versus-logistic interval crosses zero, so extra complexity has no demonstrated ranking advantage. The combined-model comparisons also change more than one factor in some cases. A useful next test must isolate the disputed factor and use new evaluation data; another point estimate from the same selected sample would not settle transfer. {{evidence:comparisons}}
